__TITLE__

Fitted path of the rig, one pose every 0.1 s, starting where the rig stood still. Other fits are drawn as thin lines for comparison. When point clouds are included, grey is the still map and the coloured points are the moving part placed with the path; doubled or smeared surfaces show where the path is wrong.

Positions are in metres in the frame of the rig at the start (z up, heading as the rig's own). The IMU alone is not shown, it drifts to tens of metres. The fit charts show the value of the fit step that covers each time.
